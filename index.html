Proposed About section

I work at the intersection of marketing strategy, operations, and innovation, helping mission-driven teams turn ambitious goals into clear plans, connected systems, and meaningful impact. My experience spans brand development, demand generation, digital and growth marketing, and the operational work that helps teams execute with focus. I’m especially interested in how strategy, cross-functional collaboration, and AI-enabled ways of working can make marketing more effective and scalable. Today, at Zearn, I help advance a mission to ensure all kids love learning math. That work calls for more than strong campaigns. It takes alignment across teams, thoughtful planning, and systems that help good ideas move from strategy into action. My leadership philosophy is people-first. I believe teams do their best work when they feel trusted, supported, and empowered to contribute. Clear priorities and strong operating rhythms matter, but so does creating a workplace where people can learn, collaborate, and do work they’re proud of. I’m always glad to connect with people working to make marketing more strategic, teams more effective, and mission-driven organizations more impactful.
